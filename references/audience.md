# Audience card

Fill one card per pitch. With several audiences, write for the person who decides and note what the others need.

```
Kind:               decision pitch | showcase
Decider:            role, name if known (showcase: the reader you most want to win)
Also in the room:   roles (and anyone who needs a different layer, e.g. engineers who'll build it)
Decision they own:  what they can approve, fund, block (showcase: adopt, clone, follow)
Wants from this:    decision | recommendation | reassurance | alignment | next step | something to admire / clone

Knows by heart      (do NOT explain)
- …

Cares about         (go one level deeper)
- …

Worries / likely objections
- …

Language & register
- language: en | vi | mixed
- form of address (vi): anh/chị/em, mình, chúng ta…
- terms they use in English: …

Format: spoken | written | slides | Figma annotation   Length: …
```

## How to fill it

- **"Knows by heart"** means things they'd be bored or insulted to hear: their own product, their metrics, standard UI patterns, their industry. When unsure, ask "would this person nod before I finished the sentence?" If yes, cut it.
- **"Cares about"** means what they're measured on and what they lose sleep over. Put it in their units, not design's.
- **Worries** means the objections you'd hear in the meeting. Answer the strongest one in the pitch.

| Role | Usually knows by heart | Usually cares about | Usual objection |
|---|---|---|---|
| Client / business owner | their market, their customers, their revenue | conversion, brand, cost, time to launch | "Will customers actually use this?" "What does it cost?" |
| Product manager | the roadmap, the metrics, the backlog | scope, impact per sprint, trade-offs | "What do we drop to fit this in?" |
| Engineering lead | the stack, the constraints, the component library | build cost, edge cases, maintenance | "Is this custom? What happens when it fails?" |
| Executive | the strategy, the competitors | risk, the one number that moves | "Why now?" |
| Other designers | patterns, heuristics, the design system | craft, consistency, rationale | "Why not the existing pattern?" |
